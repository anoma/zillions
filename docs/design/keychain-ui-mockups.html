Zillions Keychain — look & feel review

Popup (380×600) and approval window (396×640), extension 0.10.0. First the current screens, then one shared visual system applied in two layout directions, then feature ideas that need no network. Typography stays Montserrat; only sizes, weights and colour change.

1 · Today

What makes it look dated: 16px rows with 20px padding and a 1px black outline (a 2015 "wireframe" pattern), a square checkbox standing in for a radio, a 30px kebab per row, 20px centred titles, 64px-tall inputs, 32px approval amounts, and five stacked full-width buttons on the account page. Everything is a bordered box, so nothing has priority.

  • Scale. The popup is 380px wide but typeset like a 540px page: 16px rows, 20px titles, 18px menu items, 22px icons.
  • Borders do all the work. Rows, inputs, address boxes, I/O cards and secondary buttons are all 1px-outlined rounded boxes. Hierarchy comes from nothing but position.
  • Controls read wrong. The "set default" radio is drawn as a square checkbox; the kebab menu hides the most-used action (open account) behind "View Keys".
  • Duplicated navigation. Header back arrow + a "Back" text button at the bottom; the settings drawer hides three items and the version behind a 65%-wide slide-in.
  • Approvals are the best part (white summary cards) but the amount is oversized, the site is buried in the second card, and the flow cards below reintroduce the outlined-box look. Network isn't shown, which product §3.4 requires.

2 · Proposed system (shared by both directions)

Same palette family as the wallet app (warm surface, off-white cards, hairline dividers, gold as the single accent, black pill primary). Rows are flat and full-width with hairlines, no outlines, no rounded hover shapes. Type scale for a 380px column: title 14/600, row 13/600, secondary 11.5/500, eyebrow 10.5/600 caps, hero amount 28/600, buttons 13/600 at 38px.

3 · Direction A — "List" lowest risk

Keeps today's structure (list → detail) and re-skins it. Row tap opens the account; the active one shows a gold dot; "Use" switches the active account without a menu. The detail page turns five buttons into two grouped lists. Mostly a restyle of KeyListItem, Input, ActionButton sizes and the header.

4 · Direction B — "Active account first" more change

The popup's main job is "copy my address / check what's connected / switch account", so the active account becomes the home screen: name, two copy chips, a QR, then the other accounts as a compact switcher. Approvals go receipt-style, with the recipient's label when it is in the address book (idea 1 below). Changes routes and the account context, not just styles.

6 · Recommendation

  • Ship the system first (section 2) under Direction A: it is a restyle of five components and the header, no route changes, and it fixes every complaint on the list. One PR.
  • Then take Home from Direction B if you like the active-account hero: it needs the QR screen and a "current tab" lookup, which are ideas 2 and 4 anyway.
  • Approvals: Direction A's receipt card now; network badge and the privacy line are cheap and close a product gap. Address-book labels (idea 1) are the biggest trust win and can follow.
  • Settings page replaces the drawer, and auto-lock (idea 3) gives it a reason to exist.
